FOLIOLETTE / PHOTO PRINT

Your photos stay yours.

You select photos through Apple's system picker. The app stores processed copies in its local draft; original library photos are not changed. Photo metadata is not copied into PDF output. Saved layouts contain sizes and page settings, not images. The app may be included in your device backup according to your Apple settings.

When you choose to share, save or print a PDF, your chosen destination receives it. That destination's policies apply. Apple processes purchases and supplies verified entitlement information. We do not receive payment-card details.

The app keeps bounded local event counts for core actions and purchases. These counts are not transmitted to us. App Store aggregate analytics and any diagnostics you choose to send to Apple are subject to Apple's settings and policies. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send; do not send private photos unless needed for your support request.

Start a new sheet to replace the current draft. Delete individual photos from a draft and delete saved layouts in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data; copies you exported and device backups must be managed separately. Old cached exports are removed during later exports. An unreadable draft may be preserved locally for recovery rather than overwritten.
