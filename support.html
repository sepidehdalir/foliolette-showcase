FOLIOLETTE / PHOTO PRINT

A little help, on paper.

Print at the size you chose

Select the same A4 or Letter paper in Foliolette and your printer. Use 100% or Actual Size; turn off Fit to Page and borderless expansion. Measure one photo before printing a batch.

Edges clipped?

Increase the page margin. Printer printable areas vary. If AirPrint does not expose scaling controls, save the PDF and print from a Mac or PC.

Restore Lifetime Pro

Open Settings or the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account. A pending purchase unlocks after Apple approves it.

Need a hand?

Email hello@arvinify.com with your iPhone/iOS version and what happened. Avoid including private photos unless necessary.
